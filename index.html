The Image of the Artist.

What makes an artist recognizable?

This interactive visual archive explores how artists have represented themselves across time—from traditional self-portraits to performances and symbolic objects. The collection includes twenty-five works organized by the visual elements they share: tools, bodies, poses, spaces, processes, and objects.

It begins with a familiar image and a few questions, then invites you to discover relationships by browsing and filtering. No particular interpretation is required.

Some works are staged photographs or performance documentation rather than conventional self-portraits.
